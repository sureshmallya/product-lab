# Product Lab

An independent initiative exploring how AI, data,
and technology can solve practical business and
everyday problems.

## Vision

Make data easier to understand, technology easier
to use, and insights easier to act on.

## Product Explorations

### 01. Data Studio

Transform raw data into meaningful, interactive
visualizations.

- CSV uploads and external data integrations
- Standard and customizable dashboard templates
- Sales, Customer Success, Marketing, and other
  business use cases
- Exploration of MCP-enabled data connectivity
- Intelligent visualization recommendations

Status: Ideation

### 02. Marketing Intelligence

Help marketers understand not just what changed
in their performance, but why it may have changed
and what actions they could take.

- Marketing performance analysis
- Trend and anomaly detection
- Contextual intelligence
- Evidence-based insights
- Actionable recommendations

Status: Ideation

### 03. API Studio

Make APIs accessible and useful without requiring
users to build applications from scratch.

- API connectivity and data exploration
- Interactive visualizations
- Custom dashboards
- Lightweight personal and business applications

Status: Ideation

## Guiding Principles

- Solve meaningful problems.
- Keep the user experience simple.
- Use AI where it adds measurable value.
- Prioritize privacy, accuracy, and transparency.
- Validate assumptions before scaling.
- Explore open-source and accessible technologies.

## Development Approach

Each product will evolve through discovery,
experimentation, design, prototyping, validation,
and continuous iteration.

This is an evolving collection of product ideas
and experiments. Features and concepts may change
as they are explored and validated.
